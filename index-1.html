Micro Projects

Project 1: Automatic Room Light using LDR

Aim: Demonstrate automatic switching of an LED in low light using a low-voltage circuit.

Components: LDR, resistors, LED, transistor or microcontroller board, breadboard and jumper wires.

Working: LDR resistance changes with light. The circuit reads the change and switches the LED at a chosen threshold.

Safety: Low-voltage DC only; never connect a breadboard to mains electricity.

Project 2: Temperature Monitor

Aim: Read a room-temperature sensor and display the value on a serial monitor or display.

Components: Compatible microcontroller board, temperature sensor, USB cable and wires.

Procedure: Connect according to the sensor datasheet, upload a sample program, record readings and compare results.

Project 3: Water-Level Indicator

Aim: Indicate low, medium and high levels with low-voltage sensors and LEDs.

Working: Sensor inputs change as water touches each probe and activate the matching indicator.

Safety: Educational low-voltage prototype only; not for drinking-water safety or mains-powered pump control.

Project report format

  1. Title page and student details
  2. Aim and objectives
  3. Block diagram and components
  4. Working principle
  5. Procedure / algorithm
  6. Observations and result
  7. Applications, limitations and future scope
  8. References
